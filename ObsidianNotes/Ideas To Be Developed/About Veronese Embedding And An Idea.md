#Algebraic-Geometry 
The idea is simple: suppose a homogeneous ideal can be generated by its elements of a fixed degree, $I=(I_d)$, and if the ring is Noetherian so we have $I=(f_1,\dots,f_r)$ where $f_i$'s are homogeneous polynomials of degree d. Then we can "pull-straight" the variety defined by $I$.

Also, could we possibly generalize the idea of Veronese embedding to any projective scheme? Here's a key fact about Veronese subrings (see [[About a Property of the Veronese Surface]]):
**Theorem.** Let $S$ be a graded commutative rings, then the natural inclusion $S^{(d)}\rightarrow S$ induces an isomorphism $\text{Proj}\,S\cong\text{Proj}\,S^{(d)}$.
Then use this we can give an embedding $\text{Proj}\,S\rightarrow\mathbb{P}^r$. See Hartshorne, Ex.II.5.13.

Then their are language of sheaves and line bundles. Hartshorne, II.7.6, III.5.2.
