#Algebraic-Geometry 
This is the solution of Hartshorne Ex. III.4.8.d.
**Proposition.** If $X$ is a quasi-projective scheme of dimension $r$ over a field $k$, then $X$ can be covered by $r+1$ open affine subsets. In particular, combining Čech cohomology, this gives another proof of $\mathrm{cd}\,(X)\leq\mathrm{dim}\,X$.

When $X$ is projective, an induction via hyperplane sections would solve the problem easily. ==To generalize one idea, summarize and grasp the essence!== Essentially, this uses the idea that $\mathcal{O}_X(1)$ is base point free (i.e. generated by global sections) and we can choose $r+1$ global sections to generate it. Furthermore, $\mathcal{O}_X(1)$ is but one of the ample divisors on $X$. Other than hyperplanes, hypersurfaces of degree $d$ (whose sections corresponds to $\mathcal{O}_X(d)$) would also do.
Also, we could embed a quasi-projective $X$ into its projective closure $\bar{X}$. And the difficulty is that one affine cover of $\bar{X}$ may not be affine when restricted to $X$. So by the slogan: ==when one construction meets a difficulty, rather than adjusting the bad construction, it's better to solve the difficulty in the first place==, the idea is to choose effective divisors avoiding $Z=\bar{X}-X$ in one ample divisor class.

Let $\mathcal{I}_Z$ be the ideal sheaf of $\bar{X}-X$ with reduced closed scheme structure. For any section $s\in H^0(\bar{X},\mathcal{O}(m))$ representing an effective divisor $Z(s$), $Z\subset Z(s)$ if and only if $s\in H^0(\bar{X},\mathcal{I}_{Z/\bar{X}}(m))$. For $s_1,\dots,s_{r+1}$, $X=\cup_i(\bar{X}-Z(s_i))$ if and only if $s_i$ generate $\mathcal{I}_Z(m)$. So now the problem is to find $r+1$ global sections of $\mathcal{I}_{Z/\bar{X}}(m)$ for some $m$ that generate it. We choose $m$ large enough so that $\mathcal{I}_{Z/\bar{X}}(m)$ is generated by its global sections. Case $r=0$ is trivial. From now on, to get $r+1$ sections, there're two manners of induction.
